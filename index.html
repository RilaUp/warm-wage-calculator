01 / 我看事情的方式01 / HOW I SEE THINGS

先看见人。
再把事情理清楚。People first.
Then, untangle things.

比起很快给出答案，我更想先听懂：大家真正在意的，是什么？Before rushing to an answer, I'd rather understand what really matters to the people involved.

面对不同的想法、零散的信息，或一件还说不清楚的事，我习惯先找出它们之间的联系。把复杂的东西变得清楚，不是为了一个漂亮的框架，而是让下一步变得可能。Different perspectives, scattered information, a problem no one can quite name yet: I look for the connections. Making things clearer isn't about a neat framework. It's about finding a possible next step.

我也喜欢接下来的那一步：找到可以一起做的人，建立信任，把想法变成一件真正能用的东西。And I like what comes after: finding people to build with, earning trust, and turning an idea into something that actually works.

家政计薪 / 日常里的小工具HOUSEHOLD PAY / AN EVERYDAY TOOL

暖薪Warm Wage01

每个月核算家政报酬，工作、休息、节假日，总有一些细节需要反复确认。我想把这些安排和计算放在一起，让双方都看得明白。Calculating household-service pay each month meant checking workdays, days off, and holidays over and over. I wanted the schedule and the calculation in one place, clear to both sides.

我在设计时在意什么What mattered to me+

不只给出一个金额，也让计算过程可以核对。用月历呈现每天的安排，用确认图帮助沟通；工具应该减少误解，而不只是省下几次计算。Not just an amount, but a calculation you can check. A calendar makes the arrangements visible; a shareable summary helps people agree. Less misunderstanding, not just less arithmetic.

训练与记录 / 从亲身经历开始TRAINING & NOTES / A PERSONAL START

回合 ROUNDOROUNDO02

轮到自己经历康复训练时，我需要一个能安排动作、陪着练习、记下反馈的工具。不必复杂，但应该记得住，让每次开始都容易一点。Going through rehabilitation myself, I needed a way to plan exercises, follow along, and record how they felt. Nothing complicated. Just something that remembers, so starting again feels easier.

我在设计时在意什么What mattered to me+

减少重复设置，让动作自然衔接，也给身体反馈留出位置。记录不是催促自己完成更多，而是看见每一次练习，尊重自己的节奏。它提供跟练与记录，不替代专业医疗建议。Less repeated setup, a natural flow between exercises, and room to note how your body feels. A record shouldn't pressure you to do more. It should make each effort visible, at your own pace. This is a training log, not a substitute for medical advice.
